Currently, we utilize GitLab Premium, but after a thorough assessment, I believe upgrading to Ultimate aligns better with our evolving needs.

1. Comparing GitLab Premium and Ultimate Features

GitLab Ultimate offers a comprehensive suite of features beyond those available in Premium. Key enhancements include:
	•	Advanced Security Capabilities: Tools like Static and Dynamic Application Security Testing, Secret Detection, and Dependency Scanning to fortify our codebase. ￼
	•	Portfolio and Value Stream Management: Enhanced tools for tracking project progress and aligning with business objectives. ￼
	•	Unlimited Guest Users: In Ultimate, Guest users do not count towards our license seat usage, allowing us to add users with minimal interaction without impacting costs.  ￼

For a detailed comparison, please refer to GitLab’s official feature comparison chart:  ￼

2. Impact of Demoting Users to ‘Guest’ Role

Transitioning certain users to the ‘Guest’ role can optimize our license utilization. In GitLab Ultimate, Guest users have limited permissions, primarily focused on viewing project content and participating in discussions. Crucially, they do not consume a license seat, offering a cost-effective solution for stakeholders or clients who require limited access.  ￼ ￼

It’s important to note that while Guest users can view project plans and blockers, they cannot modify data they haven’t created. Their permissions are designed to provide basic visibility without compromising sensitive information.  ￼ ￼

3. Financial Considerations

Our current investment in 40 Premium seats totals approximately $9,600 annually (40 seats x $24/seat/month x 12 months). Upgrading to 20 Ultimate seats at $29 per seat per month would amount to $6,960 annually (20 seats x $29/seat/month x 12 months). This transition not only offers advanced features but also allows us to add unlimited Guest users without additional costs, potentially enhancing collaboration without significant financial impact.

4. Leveraging GitLab’s AI Companion: GitLab Duo

GitLab has introduced GitLab Duo, an AI-driven suite of features designed to enhance productivity across the software development lifecycle. Capabilities include code suggestions, code explanations, and advanced troubleshooting assistance. Integrating GitLab Duo can streamline our workflows and accelerate development cycles.  ￼

5. Exploring Elasticsearch Integration

While integrating Elasticsearch could enhance our search capabilities within GitLab, it introduces additional complexity and resource requirements. Given our current infrastructure, it may be prudent to focus on maximizing the benefits of GitLab Ultimate and GitLab Duo before considering Elasticsearch integration.

6. Proposed Implementation Strategy

Our approach can be outlined in the following phases:
	•	Phase 1: Evaluate the compatibility of GitLab Ultimate with our existing workflows and infrastructure.
	•	Phase 2: Upgrade to GitLab Ultimate and strategically assign users to appropriate roles, including ‘Guest’ where applicable.
	•	Phase 3: Integrate and train our team on GitLab Duo to enhance development efficiency.
	•	Phase 4: After optimizing the above tools, assess the feasibility and benefits of integrating Elasticsearch.
